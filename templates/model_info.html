Understanding the Metrics

Accuracy

The percentage of all test predictions that were correct.

Precision

Among customers predicted to churn, precision measures how many actually churned.

Recall

Measures how effectively the model identifies customers who actually churn.

F1 Score

Combines precision and recall into a single balanced metric.

ROC-AUC

Measures how well the model separates churned and non-churned customers across classification thresholds.
